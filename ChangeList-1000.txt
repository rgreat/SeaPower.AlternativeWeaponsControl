Version 1.5.0
- Supports Sea Power 0.9.0.
- Automatic use switches follow the unit's Standing Orders (auto-engage rules): a switch the rules forbid is amber; a click adds an allowing rule.
- Fire shows the range against the contact under the cursor (a SAM against a ship is shorter).
- Click on a target orders every gun ammunition that reaches it (option, on).
- Guns that idled near maximum range get a new aiming error (option, on).
- Guns on local control no longer wait for shell splashes (option, on).
- Guns tab: director presets (Base / Advanced / WW2 Mod), weak directors boost, solution quality and speed, aiming accuracy; gun tooltips show the director and the aiming error.
- Custom accuracy and power table (off): add or remove any gun ammunition.
- Fixed: "Guns: change maximum accuracy" now changes the real error, not only the tooltip.
