Alternative Weapons Control

Version 1.3.0:

New
- Settings open from the main menu and the pause menu (one shared "Alternative Mods" entry with several of the author's mods).
- Guns tab: table of 47 gun ammunition types - accuracy and power, with the mod's and the game's values; edit, reset. Option "Guns: alter accuracy and power" (on).
- Fire zones are red with a weak red fill on the Tactical Display map (Sea Power 0.8.7) and in 3D.
- Status "Empty" (grey) for missile launchers out of missiles.

Changed
- Special effects (flashes, fire light, binoculars view, machine gun and hit sound fixes) moved to Alternative Special Effects; settings are taken over.
- Settings that depend on a switch are hidden while it is off.
- SAM: the ready and Reload buttons no longer keep a launcher reloading after its last missile.
- Less memory churn in the Weapons tab.

Fixed
- Fire zones flickered with a second unit window open.
