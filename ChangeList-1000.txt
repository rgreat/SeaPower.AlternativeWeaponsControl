Version 1.4.0
- CIWS and machine guns also turn towards targets outside their firing sector.
- Option "Engage targets outside the firing sector" (on).
- Option "Remember earlier targets" (on): after a new fire order, a gun that cannot reach the new target keeps firing at an older one.
- Option "Bottom panel weapons act as Fire" (off): a click there shows the red firing zones.
- Group titles show all target types: "ANTI-SHIP/ASW". Option for SAM groups (off).
- Guns with a magazine show the rounds left in it.
- Fixed: land attack missiles were listed under ANTI-SHIP; the AMMO column was too narrow for "On launcher 2/2".
- Fixed: guns were sometimes pulled back to an older target instead of the newest one; firing sectors from the bottom panel were missing for guns.
- Fixed: ship guns flickering between targets when the target was lost; group Stop leaving gun Stop buttons lit.
- Groups with several ammunition types have common Fire / Stop / Reload buttons (Fire uses the chosen ammunition).
