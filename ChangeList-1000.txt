Version 1.8.0
- New: Strict EMCON switch next to Free / Tight / Hold. It turns the ship's radars off and none can be turned on. Radar directors of guns keep working as silent optical ones: the enemy does not hear them, but they are less accurate, poor at night and see only a limited range.
- New option: SAM on Tight fires only when the aircraft is inside the missile's no-escape zone.
- New launcher statuses: Assessing n%, Waiting for sensor, Sensor inoperable.
- Guns and other weapons that need no sensors no longer show grey "Sensors Offline".
- Fixed: FPS drop caused by the mod.
- Fixed: empty ammunition cells of aircraft hardpoints.
- Overlapping firing arcs are drawn without a dark red patch.
