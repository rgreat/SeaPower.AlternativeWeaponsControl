Version 1.9.0
- One settings window for all four Alternative mods:
  - Find any option by typing its name in the search box.
  - Switch between the mods' settings with one click; your edits are kept.
  - Choose the language once for all mods.
  - Undo changes you have not applied yet.
- Hints of Free / Hold buttons, the On Tight tick and launcher chips list the Standing Orders rules behind them; long hints stay while the mouse is over them.
- New option "Weapons that no Standing Orders rule covers": from which weapon status such a weapon fires by itself (it never did before).
- Fixed: deleting an attack order (X) now fully cancels it.
- Game beta 0.9.3: the "On Tight: engage hostile aircraft" tick is back; SAM charging counts the warm-up again.
