ALTERNATIVE WEAPONS CONTROL 1.2.2 - CHANGE LIST

CHANGES (since 1.2.1)

New

- Fire zones are drawn red. When you click Fire, the firing arcs are red with a weak red fill, on the Tactical Display map and in the 3D view. (The map colour needs Sea Power 0.8.7; on 0.8.6 only the 3D fill changes.)
- New status "Empty" for missile launchers that are out of missiles. It is grey, like a disabled launcher. Before, such a launcher kept saying "Ready" or "Reloading".
- New option Effects / FlashLimit (on by default): all the gun, missile and hit flashes alive at once share a total brightness limit, so many flashes together no longer blow out into white and pink glare.
- New option Effects / DayBrightness (0.1 - 1, default 0.5): flashes and fire light are dimmed by the sun, so they do not over-expose by day. Night is not affected.

Changed

- SAM: the "ready" and Reload buttons no longer keep a launcher reloading after its last missile. The rails are loaded again only for a real reason (an order with a live target, the game's own preparation, an unidentified aircraft closing in, or auto charge).
- Less memory churn and smaller load on the interface: the Weapons tab no longer creates new brushes and transforms on every refresh, and per-frame lookups in the mod no longer allocate.

Fixed

- Fire zones flickered when a second unit status window was open.

All new texts are translated into the 9 game languages.
