ALTERNATIVE WEAPONS CONTROL 1.2.1 - CHANGE LIST

CHANGES (since 1.2.0)

Fixed

- Sea Power 0.8.7: the launcher switches of the Weapons tab (and the Fire button of one launcher) were applied only to some of the game's target selections, because the game now picks launchers through a new method. They apply everywhere again.
- Sea Power 0.8.7: the light that follows a launched missile no longer fails because of a changed game method.
- Fire in an ammunition card of a single launcher now fires from that launcher (it could fire from another one when the launcher was empty).
- The mod works with Sea Power 0.8.6 as before.
