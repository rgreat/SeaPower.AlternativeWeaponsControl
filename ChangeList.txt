ALTERNATIVE WEAPONS CONTROL 1.2.0 - CHANGE LIST

CHANGES (since 1.1.0)

New

- SAM launchers work as one state machine: cold, loading, warming up, ready. Hot missiles are wanted when the readiness button is on, or the unit has an order with a live target, or the game’s own AI is preparing for an air threat, or an unidentified aircraft is closing in.
- New SAM statuses: “Rails empty”, “Loading rails”, “Unloading rails” and “Unloading: mm:ss” (the pause after an unloading). They use the Reloading colour and count as reloading in the group summary.
- Reload button on a SAM launcher: the rails are unloaded with the animation, then loaded again after a pause of half the reload cycle. With empty rails it loads at once. The button is grey while the launcher is busy, and repeated clicks do nothing.
- Unloading with the missile button also starts the pause, and the next load waits for it.
- A hot launcher that has fired its last missile loads the next ones by itself while they are needed. Otherwise it goes cold, with no pointless reload count.
- The load animation always opens the reload.
- The game’s own warm-up for an air target is no longer cancelled by the mod.
- “Disable AI cheating” (AI tab, default on): your ships on Free engage contacts marked hostile on the plot regardless of their true side. On Tight with “On Tight: engage hostile aircraft” the same works for aircraft and helicopters only.
- Section title rows with several groups have common Fire, Stop and Reload and target-type switches.
- The Reload tooltip names the ammunition the game reloads.
- Default section order is now SAM, GUNS, ANTI-SHIP, ASW; the other sections follow in the usual order.

Changed

- SAM auto-charge counts the closing speed against your ship’s own movement.
- Less load on the game: cached reflection lookups, and SAM hooks skip launchers that cannot warm up.
- Debug traces for SAM loading, the AI’s possible targets, Fire clicks and pinned launchers.

Fixed

- CIWS and machine guns now aim at targets beyond maximum range. The hook was looking in the wrong class.
- The missile button no longer shows ready while the missiles are still loading.
- “Rails empty” is shown instead of “Ready” for empty rails.


ALTERNATIVE WEAPONS CONTROL 1.1.0 - CHANGE LIST

NEW

- Weapons tab for ground vehicles and land installations, and for aircraft and helicopters. Both can be turned off in the settings.
- SAM readiness button: load the missiles onto the rails now, or put them away. They are put away automatically after 15 minutes. Missiles stay loaded after a launch, and a hot launcher loads the next ones by itself while there are targets or an aircraft threat; with nothing on the rails and no need it goes cold.
- SAM auto-charge when an unknown aircraft approaches. Works for AI ships too.
- "On Free: engage unidentified aircraft" switch on each unit. It engages fast (500 kn+) unidentified aircraft heading towards the ship within 20 nm. The switch does not know whose aircraft it is. Default is off for your ships and on for enemy AI.
- Fire button shows weapon firing arcs in red on the map and in 3D while the target is being picked. The game's own maximum-range circle is hidden for it, and the game's own Fire buttons keep their usual look.
- Guns, CIWS and machine guns aim at targets beyond maximum range without firing.
- Drag sections (SAM, GUNS, ...) to reorder them.
- Ammunition selector in groups with several ammo types, with its own target-type switches and Cease fire per ammunition.
- Ship chase options: a ship keeps its course instead of running after RBU targets or aircraft to get them into range.
- Settings window with tabs: Weapons panel, Fire Control, AI, Guns, Visual effects, Debug, Language.
- Localization into 9 languages (en, ru, cn, de, es, fr, ko, ja, vn). The lang/<code>.ini files can be edited.
- Gun accuracy option for close range (MaxAccuracyMeters).

VISUAL AND OPTICS

- Light effects: battles look more alive. Gunfire, hits, missile launches and detonations light up their surroundings in 3D, and the bigger the weapon, the brighter, wider and longer the flash. Fires on ships flicker with a living glow. Every effect can be switched off, and brightness is adjustable.
  - Muzzle flashes grow with the size of the shell.
  - Missile boosters cast a light that follows the missile, stronger for heavier missiles.
  - Hit flashes follow the size of the explosion.
  - Torpedoes, bombs and ASW mortars flash when they detonate.
- Machine guns: tracers and the muzzle burst sound now last for the whole burst.
- Binoculars start in the normal view at maximum zoom, with no night or thermal filter by default (option: only by day).

FIXED

- Machine-gun tracers stopping after about 1.1 s, and the gap in the middle of a long burst sound.
- Missing explosion sound on some gun hits.
