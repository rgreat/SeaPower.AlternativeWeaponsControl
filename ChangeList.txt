Version 1.4.0
- CIWS and machine guns now also turn towards a target that is outside their firing sector, as far as they can.
- New option "Engage targets outside the firing sector" (Guns tab, on): big guns take such targets too and turn towards them. Works for ships loaded after the change.
- New option "Remember earlier targets" (Guns tab, on): when you give a new fire order, the guns switch to the new target, but the old targets are not forgotten. A gun that cannot reach the new target (too far, or outside its sector) keeps firing at an older one it can reach, and goes back to the newest when it can. Cease fire clears the list.
- New option "Bottom panel weapons act as Fire" (UI tab, off): a click on a weapon in the panel at the bottom of the screen works like the Fire button of the Weapons tab - it shows the firing zones in red.
- Group titles show all target types of their missiles, e.g. "ANTI-SHIP/ASW" for the URK-5. New option "SAM group title lists other target types" (UI tab, off) does the same for anti-aircraft missiles that can also hit ships ("SAM/ANTI-SHIP").
- "Section" is now called "group" in the texts.
- Guns with a magazine show the rounds left in the magazine, not in the mount.
- Fixed: land attack missiles (such as the Tomahawk RGM-109C) were listed under ANTI-SHIP.
- Fixed: the AMMO column was too narrow for "On launcher 2/2".
- Fixed: "Remember earlier targets": guns that could reach both targets were sometimes pulled back to the older target instead of the newest one, so a turret did not fire at the second target.
- Fixed: with "Bottom panel weapons act as Fire" the firing sectors on the map were not shown for some weapons (guns).
- Fixed: with "Remember earlier targets" a ship's guns kept switching back and forth every half second (statuses flickered) when the target was no longer in contact.
- Groups with several ammunition types now have common Fire, Stop and Reload buttons (Fire uses the chosen ammunition, or the one that is left).
- Fixed: group Stop could leave the Stop buttons of single guns lit.
