Version 1.5.0
- Supports Sea Power 0.9.0 (older versions are no longer supported).
- Automatic use per launcher now follows the unit's Standing Orders (the game's auto-engage rules). A switch is amber when the rules forbid that weapon against that target type; a click adds a rule to the unit's Standing Orders that allows it. Changes made in the game's own Standing Orders editor are picked up at once.
- Fire shows the range against the contact under the cursor, as the game works it out (a SAM against a ship is shorter than against an aircraft).
- New option "Click on a target: all guns" (Guns tab, on): a right click on a contact orders every gun ammunition that reaches it, not only one.
- New option "Unstick guns at long range" (Guns tab, on): a gun whose aiming error put the aim point beyond its firing table, so it idled for minutes, gets a new error.
- New option "Local control: no spotting wait" (Guns tab, on): a gun without a working director fires at its normal rate instead of waiting for each shell to land.
- Your orders go first: a Fire order or a click on a contact now takes the guns over, also after loading a saved game.
- Guns tab of the settings: director presets (Base / Advanced / WW2 Mod), "Weak directors boost", "Solution quality" with optical and radar directors and without a director, "Firing solution build speed", "Shell accuracy", "Aiming accuracy". Gun tooltips show the director, the firing solution and the aiming error at the target's range.
- New option "Custom accuracy and power" (Guns tab, off): a table of gun ammunition where you can change accuracy and power, remove a row and add any other gun ammunition of the game from a drop-down list with a filter.
- Settings regrouped: the tab with the SAM options is called SAM, the director and accuracy settings are on a new Fire Control tab.
- Fixed: "Guns: change maximum accuracy" changed only the tooltip; now it changes the real aiming error at close range too (default 5 m, the game has about 40 m).
- All texts are translated into the nine languages.
