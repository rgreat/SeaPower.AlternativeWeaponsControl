ALTERNATIVE WEAPONS CONTROL 1.3.0 - CHANGE LIST

New

- Settings can be opened from the main menu and the pause menu. With several of the author's mods installed there is one shared "Alternative Mods" entry.
- New table in the "Guns" tab of the settings window (shown only while "Guns: alter accuracy and power" is on): accuracy and warhead power of 47 gun ammunition types (the author's pack, ammunition_fix.txt in the mod folder) with the game's names and the game's own values. Edit any value, reset a row or all to the game's values. Option Guns / AmmunitionFix (on by default); "Gun maximum accuracy" moved to this tab too. Applied to ammunition loaded after Apply (a mission start).

Changed

- Special effects (flashes, fire light, binoculars normal view, machine gun and hit sound fixes) moved to the mod Alternative Special Effects; install it to keep them. Settings are taken over on its first start.
- The settings window no longer has the "Visual effects" tab, and the Guns tab no longer has the "Tracers and sound" group.
