Version 2.0.1
- Aircraft (stable game 0.9.3):
  - Fixed: a fighter with air-to-air missiles turned on ignored your right click on an air target. Its AI makes no engage task for a target that the launcher's own sensors do not see, so the fighter never flew to it; and the game dropped the clicked target as soon as it was out of reach and the aircraft was not closing in. Now the clicked target is kept (until it is destroyed or you cancel it) and the launcher's vision requirement is waived while the AI plans the engagement (the launch itself still needs the target to be seen): the fighter flies to the target and fires its missiles. Option "Fighters: keep a clicked air target" (AI tab), on by default.

Version 2.0.0
- Aircraft:
  - Weapons tab (beta game only): "Max-rate turns" button (after Strict EMCON): overrides the unit's Standing Orders "Aircraft turns out of combat" and "Aircraft turns in combat" with Max rate and lifts the limits of route turns (bank cap, turn limit) and of the combat turns (sustained / energy limited); saved with the unit. The arrow opens 4 levels of the minimum speed for the extra maneuverability out of combat (Limit / Low / Optimal / High, shown in knots; default Optimal); the aircraft turns like the game's Max rate mode below it. A box next to it shows the current load factor (+X.XG / -X.XG).
  - New: aircraft whose missiles you turned off pursue and shoot with their cannon by themselves (before, the AI kept waiting for a missile shot). Option "Fighters: guns when missiles are off" (AI tab).
  - Fixed: the AI of an aircraft no longer wipes your waiting orders (for instance a gun order that waits for the target to come into range) when it picks its own target.
  - Fixed: the rounds of a CIWS with a magazine (aircraft cannons) were shown as 0 in the Weapons tab; the rounds in the mount are counted now.

Version 1.9.0
- Settings window (the same window and rules in all four Alternative mods):
  - One-line tab strip with arrows; the mouse wheel scrolls it.
  - Search box: finds options by their names (not by descriptions); the X clears it; the text stays when you switch to another mod's window.
  - The tab and every group have a back button (undo the edits not applied yet).
  - The row of the installed mods at the top of the window: a click opens that mod's window (the edits are kept). Language is now one drop-down at its right for all mods ("Default" = the game's language).
  - Ctrl+C copies the help text of the option under the mouse.
  - The settings window opens on the mod used last (menu button; the gear of the World View window).
- Debug: "Screenshot all tabs now" (all installed Alternative mods, BepInEx/<Mod>_screens).
- Game terms in the texts are written as in the game's own language files.

Version 1.8.0
- Beta game: the "On Tight: engage hostile aircraft" tick is back (the release game has it as a doctrine item). The tick reads what the unit's Standing Orders say (do all its air-capable weapons engage at Weapons Tight); a click puts a rule on top of the unit's own Standing Orders (a local override), another click takes it out. Amber while the rule overrides the other rules or only some weapons engage.
- Beta game 0.9.3: SAM charging (Ready button, auto-charge) counts the warm-up after the load again, as in the release game (the beta no longer counts it by itself); the launcher is not usable during the count.
- Beta game: the hint of the On Tight box lists the numbered Standing Orders rules that decide the ship's air launchers against aircraft (the box's own rule and the ones it overrides).
- Beta game: the hints of the amber Free / Hold buttons list the numbered Standing Orders rules behind it (Always rules for Hold, Never rules for Free).
- Beta game: new option "Weapons that no Standing Orders rule covers" (Settings, Fire: Free / Tight / Hold = from which weapon status such a weapon fires by itself; default Free). Before, such a weapon never fired by itself.
- Beta game: the hint of a launcher chip lists the Standing Orders rules that apply to it (number, policy, domains | weapons -> targets, conditions; in the game's own words), or says that no rule covers the weapon.
- Fixed: the hints of the Weapons tab stay while the mouse is over them (they disappeared after a few seconds).
- Fixed: X on the map (deleting an attack order that is already being carried out) now cancels it like Stop: the weapons firing it stop, not only the orders still waiting.
- New: "Strict EMCON" switch next to Free / Tight / Hold: turns the unit's radars off and none can be turned on, even to engage a target (saved with the unit).
- The header buttons and ticks light up under the mouse; the radars Strict EMCON turned off come back on when it ends.
- New: Strict EMCON no longer switches off radar directors that serve only guns: they work as silent optical directors (not heard by ESM; accuracy between local control and the radar, setting Guns/StrictEmconOpticalShare; no shell tracking; night by the rules of optics, splashes unseen in the dark; targets only within the range of optics); the directors window and the gun tooltip say "radar off: optical mode".
- Fixed: deleting an attack order on the map (X: sonobuoy pattern points, bombing points) left its tasks in the unit's list and the unit went on with them; now they are removed with the order.
- Fixed: the mod lowered FPS (big-gun local solution asked on every frame of every gun; director profile hook ran on every call; the settings file was checked on disk every second - no longer). Fixed: the bottom-panel fire zones kept the units of earlier missions in memory.
- New option "On Tight: fire only in no-escape zone" (Fire; off): the ships' automatic SAM fire on Weapons Tight waits until the aircraft is inside the missile's no-escape zone.
- Settings: the Fire Control tab has two inner tabs, General and Directors.
- Fixed: the ammunition rows of a group (e.g. depth bombs and sonobuoys of an aircraft's hardpoint) show their own stock, not an empty cell.
- Fire: identical or nearly identical firing arcs on the map share one red fill instead of stacking into an opaque red; coinciding 3D wedges (torpedo tubes of a submarine) are drawn once.
- New launcher statuses: "Assessing n%" and "Waiting for sensor" (an order waits because the crew has not assessed the target yet - the game's reaction time; slow for old ships), "Sensor inoperable" (all directors destroyed; the game said "Ready").
- Weapons that need no sensors (guns, RBU, bombs) no longer show grey "Sensors Offline" when their directors are off.

Version 1.6.0
- LOADER CHANGED: the mod now needs R.A.T.S. Keel (recommended) or Anchor Chain; before, only Anchor Chain. See Install in the README.
- Supports Sea Power 0.9.2 (0.9.0 and later).
- New option "Fire at a point of the map" (Guns tab, on): a right click on the empty map while guns are being aimed puts an invisible decoy on that point and the guns engage it like a target. The cursor is grey over the map, the order leaves a log line and a voice reply, and the targeting ends after the click like for a real target.
- Fixed: turning a forbidden SUR / SUB / LND switch On let the ship fire on Weapons Tight (saves with such rules are corrected on load); now only on Weapons Free.
- The AIR / MSL / SUR / SUB / LND switches now go On, Off, then back to the unit's Standing Orders (an amber "off by Standing Orders" switch when they forbid it).
- The Reload button of an ammunition reloads that ammunition, not the one the gun happened to have selected.
- "Bottom panel weapons act as Fire" is on by default.
- The "Alternative Mods" menu button also works when the mod is started by R.A.T.S. Keel.
- All texts are translated into the nine languages.

Compatibility with the WW2 mod was worked on; it is recommended to use it without its optional dll. For it:
- New option "Scatter for every barrel of a turret" (Guns tab, on): each barrel of a twin or triple turret draws its own shell scatter, so the shells no longer fly in one line.
- New option "Big guns build a solution without a director" (Guns tab, on): a naval gun above about 50 mm with no working director builds its firing solution itself (poorer than a director). Guns without any sensor use the mission's average director.
- Fire Control tab: "Solution lost when the target manoeuvres" is now a percent of the current solution (10 %), new "Solution build speed without a director" (75 % of the best director).
- New defaults, the WW2 Mod preset: "Weak directors boost" 10 %, "Solution quality without a director" 0.5, "Firing solution build speed" 0.5. Your own settings are kept.

Version 1.5.0
- Supports Sea Power 0.9.0 (older versions are no longer supported).
- Automatic use per launcher now follows the unit's Standing Orders (the game's auto-engage rules). A switch is amber when the rules forbid that weapon against that target type; a click adds a rule to the unit's Standing Orders that allows it. Changes made in the game's own Standing Orders editor are picked up at once.
- Fire shows the range against the contact under the cursor, as the game works it out (a SAM against a ship is shorter than against an aircraft).
- New option "Click on a target: all guns" (Guns tab, on): a right click on a contact orders every gun ammunition that reaches it, not only one.
- New option "Unstick guns at long range" (Guns tab, on): a gun whose aiming error put the aim point beyond its firing table, so it idled for minutes, gets a new error.
- New option "Local control: no spotting wait" (Guns tab, on): a gun without a working director fires at its normal rate instead of waiting for each shell to land.
- Your orders go first: a Fire order or a click on a contact now takes the guns over, also after loading a saved game.
- Guns tab of the settings: director presets (Base / Advanced / WW2 Mod), "Weak directors boost", "Solution quality" with optical and radar directors and without a director, "Firing solution build speed", "Shell accuracy", "Aiming accuracy". Gun tooltips show the director, the firing solution and the aiming error at the target's range.
- New option "Custom accuracy and power" (Guns tab, off): a table of gun ammunition where you can change accuracy and power, remove a row and add any other gun ammunition of the game from a drop-down list with a filter.
- Settings regrouped: the tab with the SAM options is called SAM, the director and accuracy settings are on a new Fire Control tab.
- Fixed: "Guns: change maximum accuracy" changed only the tooltip; now it changes the real aiming error at close range too (default 5 m, the game has about 40 m).
- All texts are translated into the nine languages.

- Chips of weapons that cannot engage a domain (RBU vs ships, torpedoes vs submarines) no longer show "forced by Standing Orders".
- Settings window restructured: Weapons panel, Auto fire (Standing Orders, unidentified aircraft, SAM), Guns (targets, fire control), Accuracy (aiming, global, ammunition, directors), AI, Language, Debug. Settings keep their values.
