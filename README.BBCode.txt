Adds a new [b]Weapons[/b] tab in the Unit Status window of your units, and a set of combat and visual improvements.

[h1]Features[/h1]
[h3]Weapons tab in Unit Status[/h3]
[olist]
[*][b]All launchers at a glance[/b], by kind (SAM, anti-ship, land attack, ASW, guns, CIWS), launchers of the same system in one row (×N), opened to every launcher and every type of ammunition. Groups with several ammunition types have an ammunition selector with its own target-type switches and Cease fire.
State of each launcher and its current target (Track N, with the name once identified).
Ammunition left / at the start, coloured by what is left.
Fire-control channels of the directors: targets tracked and guided weapons in flight at a time; point at them for each director, with a link to its reference.
Links to the reference of every launcher and weapon.
Drag a section title (SAM, GUNS, …) to reorder the sections.
[*][b]Ground vehicles, land installations, aircraft and helicopters[/b] get the tab too (each can be switched off in the settings).
[*][b]Fire orders[/b] for a group, an ammunition or one launcher: pick the target on the map, salvo and launch options, cease fire, reload. A launcher given its own target fires at it, waits while it is busy or no fire-control channel is free, and does not take other launchers off their targets. Kept in saved games.
[*][b]Automatic use[/b] per launcher and target domain (aircraft, missiles, surface ships, submarines, land): switch off what the AI may use by itself - e.g. keep the long-range SAMs for aircraft and leave missiles to the short-range ones. Merged with the unit's Standing Orders; kept in saved games. Manual fire is never restricted.
[*][b]Weapons status[/b] (Free / Tight / Hold) with "On Tight: engage hostile aircraft" and "On Free: engage unidentified aircraft" at the top of the tab. The second one engages fast (500 kn+) unidentified aircraft heading towards the ship within 20 nm. It does not know whose aircraft it is. Default: off for your ships, on for enemy AI.
[*]A legend behind the (i) next to the tab's name.
[/olist]

[h3]SAM launchers[/h3]
[olist]
[*][b]Readiness button:[/b] load the missiles onto the rails now, or put them away. They are put away automatically after 15 minutes if unused. Missiles stay loaded after a launch.
[*][b]Auto-charge[/b] when an unknown aircraft approaches. Works for AI ships too.
[/olist]

[h3]Fire and guns[/h3]
[olist]
[*][b]Fire shows the weapon's firing arcs[/b] in red on the map and in 3D while the target is being picked. The game's own maximum-range circle is hidden for it. The game's own Fire buttons keep their usual look.
[*][b]Aim beyond maximum range:[/b] guns, CIWS and machine guns turn towards a target out of range without firing.
[*][b]Close-range accuracy:[/b] option for the best accuracy of guns (default 5 m).
[*][b]Ship chase options:[/b] a ship keeps its course instead of running after RBU targets or aircraft to get them into range.
[/olist]

[h3]Fire control (optional)[/h3]
[olist]
[*][b]Guns take one director.[/b] A gun opening fire takes a channel on one of its directors, not on all of them as in the game, so the others stay free for other guns and for missiles sharing them. On by default.
[*][b]Guns without a free channel aim on local control[/b] (less accurate, worse at night) until a channel frees. Off by default.
[/olist]

[h3]Visual and optics[/h3]
[olist]
[*][b]Light effects:[/b] battles look more alive. Gunfire, hits, missile launches and detonations light up their surroundings in 3D, and the bigger the weapon, the brighter, wider and longer the flash. Fires on ships flicker with a living glow. Every effect can be switched off, and brightness is adjustable.
Muzzle flashes grow with the size of the shell.
Missile boosters cast a light that follows the missile, stronger for heavier missiles.
Hit flashes follow the size of the explosion.
Torpedoes, bombs and ASW mortars flash when they detonate.
[*][b]Machine guns:[/b] tracers and the muzzle burst sound now last for the whole burst.
[*][b]Binoculars[/b] start in the normal view at maximum zoom, with no night or thermal filter by default (option: only by day).
[/olist]

[h3]Settings and languages[/h3]
[olist]
[*]Settings window in the game with tabs: Weapons panel, Fire Control, AI, Guns, Visual effects, Debug, Language.
[*]Localized into 9 languages (en, ru, cn, de, es, fr, ko, ja, vn). The [b]lang/<code>.ini[/b] files can be edited to translate or change texts.
[/olist]
